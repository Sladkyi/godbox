The world now has a voice and a memory.

Ambient sound follows the land — meadow, forest, war, fire — with stings for birth, death, lightning, and howls. The chronicle writes stories, not logs: scouts come home with a tale, a theft becomes a debt, rain on a hungry town can make you its patron.

An invisible director foreshadows trouble, then injects it: wolves, drought, a gift to a weaker people. Towns remember grudges, trade surplus, and ally against a common enemy. Wars still grow from heat — raids, sieges, refugees — and you remain a god, not a general.
